Monthly Office Snapshot

One row per office × service line (PC). Total Billed is accrual charges, not cash collected — no-fault/WC claims settle 12–36 months out, so “Billed − Fixed Cost” is a coverage indicator, not profit. Rent is fixed monthly per service line; Payroll = Days × Payroll/Day. Outcome Assessment (Allegiance) and Pain Mgmt (Max Gilles MD) run within existing service days and carry no standalone footprint. Pts/Day bands: green ≥ 10, yellow 5–10, red < 5.

Issues & Flagged Rows

Full census of every flagged row for the month, grouped by office: doc-blocked (No claim / insurance / demo / employer / address), Not billable or fundable, No consult, repeated tests, and processing errors (mixed tests, unknown PCs). Amounts shown are the row's charges; rows marked EXCL are excluded from all dashboard totals, all others remain in the gross totals.
